Scoring criteria
  • Each of the five pillars contributes 20% of the Movement Score.
  • Left and right sides are scored separately for ankle loading, landing control, and hip mobility.
  • The radar point uses the average of the two sides. Either side can still trigger a deficiency flag.
  • Side-to-side gaps are shown separately and do not reduce the score a second time.
  • If a pillar is left blank, the score averages only the pillars completed on that assessment.
  • Thresholds are coaching benchmarks—not a diagnosis or an injury-risk prediction.

Values are read off the video (use your V6-3D pose viewer or a protractor overlay to read the angle). Auto-measurement from pose tracking is the next layer to add.
